Booberman sound effects: Kenney, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
=====================================================================

Every .ogg file in this folder is a byte-for-byte copy of a sound effect from an official Kenney
starter-kit repository (https://github.com/KenneyNL). The .mp3 files are only a re-encode of the
matching .ogg (mono, libmp3lame -q:a 4) so browsers without Ogg Vorbis support (older Safari / iOS)
can play the same sound. Nothing else was changed: no editing, mixing or pitch shifting on disk.
